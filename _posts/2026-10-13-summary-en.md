Google DeepMind has released Gemini 4 \(codenamed &quot;Argon&quot;\) through its API at an introductory price of $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input tokens 95% cheaper. Early API users report strong writing quality and SVG output. Gemini 4 Argon is a new flagship model from one of the largest AI labs, so its launch shifts competition among frontier models on both capability and cost. According to a commenter citing the Arena Pareto-frontier leaderboard, it offers similar intelligence to rival models at a much lower cost per task. hackernews · 737max · Oct 13, 16:03 · [Discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=50073879)

**Background**: Gemini is Google DeepMind&\#x27;s family of large language models, and each new generation is offered through an API that charges per million tokens, where a token is a short chunk of text.
