rss · The Verge - AI · Oct 19, 13:58

**「Impact」** Organizations that run Cairn should confirm that their instances are not publicly reachable, since the attacker reached targets through exposed deployments. Companies that rely on third-party vendors&\#x27; websites should also ask those vendors whether they were affected.

### [Anthropic&\#x27;s Junior Technical Hiring Falls as AI Agents Take on More Work](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-has-nearly-stopped-hiring-junior-staff-because-ai-agents-now-work-six-times-as-many-hours/) ⭐️ 8.0/10

rss · The Decoder · Oct 18, 13:22

**「The cognitive commons concept」** The article&\#x27;s labor argument draws on Nolan Lovett&\#x27;s conceptual paper &quot;The Tragedy of the Cognitive Commons,&quot; which applies the commons-depletion idea to professional expertise. Lovett argues that individually rational AI adoption decisions can collectively deplete the shared pool of internalized expertise that professions need to renew themselves. He also identifies a &quot;Validation Tether,&quot; meaning AI orchestration depends on humans who have already mastered the underlying work.

**「Why it matters」** For early-career workers in technical roles, the study indicates that entry-level openings are shrinking even at a company that is growing quickly, with the steepest declines in roles where Claude handles the most complex work. The report also says human attention and judgment have become a bottleneck, as meeting time per employee rose 20% year over year and 16% for technical contributors.
