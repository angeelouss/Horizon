This attack allows malicious prompts embedded in one document to be executed by Copilot and then copied into subsequent documents, creating new carriers. This discovery represents a new class of AI security vulnerability, as it&\#x27;s the first known prompt injection attack that enables self-replication, posing a significant threat to data integrity and user privacy within enterprise environments. The ability for malicious instructions to spread autonomously across documents could lead to widespread data manipulation or exfiltration without direct user intervention. The attack leverages hidden instructions, such as white-on-white text, embedded within a source document that Copilot for Word processes.
