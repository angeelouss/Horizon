Tencent has released Hy3, a new 295B-parameter Mixture-of-Experts \(MoE\) language model under an Apache 2.0 license, featuring 21B active parameters and 3.8B MTP layer parameters. rss · Simon Willison · Jul 6, 23:57

**Background**: Mixture-of-Experts \(MoE\) is an AI technique that uses multiple specialized sub-models, or &quot;experts,&quot; each trained on different aspects of data, with a &quot;gate&quot; network deciding which expert\(s\) to use for a given input. This allows for models with a very large total number of parameters but only a subset of them active during inference, offering a balance between capacity and computational efficiency. Quantization, specifically FP8 in this context, is a technique used in large language models to reduce their size and computational requirements by converting high-precision floating-point numbers to lower-precision formats, making models faster and less memory-intensive for deployment.
